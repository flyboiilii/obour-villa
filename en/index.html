Qualifies for Egyptian citizenship by investment

  • Minimum property purchase of USD 300,000 per investor.
  • Purchase funds wired from outside Egypt.
  • Property registered in the real estate register. This property already is.
  • Joint ownership allowed where each co-investor's share is at least USD 300,000.
  • The property is held for at least 5 years. Early sale triggers extra obligations.

Questions buyers ask

Can a foreign national buy this property?

Yes. The title is registered in Shahr El Aqary for land and building, which is the form of ownership a foreign buyer needs. Most Orabi listings are association allocations or green contracts and do not offer this.

How does the citizenship route work?

Egypt's citizenship by investment programme accepts a registered property purchase of at least USD 300,000 per investor, paid with funds wired from abroad and held for at least 5 years. This property is already registered. Final eligibility is confirmed by the Citizenship by Investment Unit.

Can brothers or several family members buy together?

Yes. Joint ownership is allowed when each co-investor's share is at least USD 300,000. At USD 1,250,000 four co-investors at USD 312,500 each all clear the threshold.

Who handles the citizenship filing?

Our legal team can prepare and file the complete application for a separate fee. The attorney is the seller's father, and the buyer is free to appoint any counsel of their own choice.

What are the payment terms?

40% on signing and 60% over 24 months, or 5% off the list price for full payment within 60 days. The villa is sold fully furnished at the list price.

I am abroad. How do I view it?

Start with a video call. We walk you through the villa and the documents, then arrange a private viewing when you travel. The pool and garden are prepared before every visit.

Which documents will I see?

The ownership summary, the Benha University survey report, the structural safety certificate, the 2014 structural and permit drawings, and the furniture inventory. Full copies go to serious buyers after a first conversation.
